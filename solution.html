Common questions

Does CareBase replace care managers?

No. It handles routine monitoring and sorting so care managers can spend their time on the patients who most need a human conversation.

Which programs does it support?

Chronic Care Management, Annual Wellness Visits, Behavioral Health Integration and Transitional Care Management.

Do patients need special equipment?

A smartphone is enough to get started. Medical devices can be connected for additional monitoring.
